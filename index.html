Project

Credit default risk on 58.4 million rows

Python, SQL and PostgreSQL, scikit-learn, XGBoost, Jupyter

  • Loaded the seven related tables of the Home Credit Default Risk dataset, 58.4 million rows in total, into PostgreSQL and indexed the join keys.
  • Judged extreme values by whether the rest of the record backed them up, not by a cut-off rule. That’s how Exhibit A turned up, along with 55,374 applicants “employed” for about 1,000 years: a placeholder for no current job, and 99.96% of them were pensioners.
  • Dropped 28 redundant housing columns (three versions each of 14 measurements, correlated at 0.97–0.998) and ten near-constant flags.
  • On the application table, baseline models reached a PR-AUC of 0.217 with logistic regression and 0.234 with untuned XGBoost, against a no-skill baseline of 0.0807.
  • Turned down a 0.002 “improvement” because cross-validation put the noise at about 0.017.

Smaller than the noise means it didn’t happen.
